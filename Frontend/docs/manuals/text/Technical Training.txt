The Main Tether is the main cable that mechanically holds the aerostat and connects the Air Carrier, to the  Mooring System. The Main Tether carries the power and data communication: receiving payload uplink  commands and transmitting downlink video images and data between the airborne carrier, via the mooring  system, to Portable   Ground Control Station (PGCS ).  The Main Tether is connected to three different parts:  1.   Main winch   –   the lower side of the   M ain   T ether is (mechanically) connected to winch's drum and  (electrically - through slip rings/rotary joints) to the AC electric box.  2.   Aerostat's Swivel   –   M ain   T ether is secured mechanically to the lower eye of the aerostat's swivel   (the  second connected to the Aerostat ’ s flying lines) .  3.   Airborne carrier   –   T he   M ain   T ether electrical connector is connected to the   A irborne   C arrier   connector.  Aerostat Main Tether  26 April 2018 37

The PGCS must be connect to the Internet for Software  upgrades, TeamViewer remote control (Remote support)  & Maps downloading.
