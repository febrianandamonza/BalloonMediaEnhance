1 Description  Page:   1-1  Date:   21.Dec.2020  Rev:   1.9  1.1 General  Epsilon 140/140Z/140LC/140Z G2 is a four sensor gyro-stabilized gimbal with direct drive torque motor technology providing stable video even at 30x optical zoom. The 140Z version has LWIR sensor with long-range continuous zoom providing up to 7.7° vertical FOV. All Epsilon 140/140Z/140LC/140Z G2 has state of the art onboard image processing capability. The onboard processing allows target tracking and eliminates any communication latency issues. It’s processed video reduces the data rate and requires lower bandwidth datalinks, which is of an extreme importance for modern   UAVs.   The   video   stream   along   with   snapshots   can   be   stored   in   HD   quality   onboard   the   Epsilon 140/140Z/140LC/140Z G2. Epsilon 140/140Z/140LC/140Z G2 is capable of hands-free tracking of moving objects. The operator can allocate the target and zoom in, while the onboard processor will hold the target in the center of the video screen, regardless of the aircraft movement direction. This is a fundamental feature for long-range surveillance, monitoring applications and law enforcement applications. Target tracking is paramount in applications where the aircraft is moving, and when the object of interest is moving relative to the ground. You can operate the Epsilon 140/140Z/140LC/140Z G2 in any conditions as it's IP64 rated. It's environmentally sealed housing will protect electronics from dust and heavy rain. The dome design is very robust and optimized for demanding applications were the gimbal needs to handle shock and rapid accelerations. Epsilon 140/140Z/140LC/140Z G2 will handle routine parachute recoveries, net recoveries, belly landings and catapult launch with ease. Electronic stabilization with roll correction, removes the high frequency jitter often found on small unmanned aircraft platforms. This significantly reduces the operator workload and allows concentrating on the mission objective. Epsilon will automatically hold the video centered on the scene, independent of the aircraft movement, even at tele- zoom setting. 3 Laser Safety  Page:   3-1  Date:   21.Dec.2020  Rev:   1.9  3.1 Important  PLEASE READ CAREFULLY!  Caution must be taken when using a laser   device due to the potential harmful effects   of staring into a laser beam.   NEVER   stare into a laser beam directly or indirectly.   DO NOT   point laser beam into the direction of someone’s eyes. Avoid direct eye exposure to the beam.  3.2 Class I  A Class 1 laser is safe under all conditions of normal use. The maximum permissible exposure cannot be exceeded when viewing a laser with the naked eye or with the aid of typical magnifying optics (e.g. telescope or microscope). It is important to realize that certain lasers classified as Class 1 may still pose a hazard when viewed with a telescope or microscope of sufficiently large aperture. An unsafe power level may be collected by a magnifying optic with larger aperture.  3.3 Class IIIb  A Class IIIb laser may cause damage if the beam enters the eye directly. Lasers in this class can cause permanent eye damage. A diffuse reflection is generally not hazardous but specular or mirror like reflections can be just as dangerous as direct exposures. Protective eyewear is recommended when direct beam viewing of Class IIIb lasers may occur.  More Information can be found here:   http://www.lasersafetyfacts.com/  Epsilon 140/140Z/140Z G2/ 140LC User Manual RT LTA Systems Ltd RT LTA Systems Ltd

5 Epsilon Control Application  Page:   5-3  Date:   11.Jun.2021  Rev:   2.0  5.3 PC Connection  There are two ways to connect to Epsilon from the computer for control it:  RS232 serial port with baud rate 57600. The USB to RS232 converter can be also used in case PC does not have direct RS232 interface. In this case, the converter provides virtual COM port interface, which is used in the same way.  Ethernet connection - the PC must be set to be in the same subnet with Epsilon. For example, Epsilon default IP is set to 192.168.10.100 and PC must have the same subnet, different last number for IP, for example, 192.168.10.11. The user can also select any other IP settings as well as or to set DHCP mode to use router. To set up your computer's network, refer to section   5.4 - PC Network configuration . Open   Window > Connect   to establish a connection to the Epsilon. The COM Settings window will appear. Select either serial or Ethernet communication (by choosing the appropriate checkbox) and press   Connect   button. For Serial connection, correct Port and Baud rate must be chosen. In the case of Ethernet communication, enter the IP address and port to which you want to connect to on the Epsilon gimbal.  COM Settings window  After communication is established, the Status indicator on the bottom will indicate green status. This means that Epsilon is responding to the packets sent from the application.  Communication status  Epsilon 140/140Z/140Z G2/ 140LC User Manual RT LTA Systems Ltd RT LTA Systems Ltd

5 Epsilon Control Application  Page:   5-17  Date:   21.Dec.2020  Rev:   1.9  5.10.2 IR Sensor  IR settings window is shown below:  IR sensor settings  AGC (Automatic Gain Control) modes   – IR sensor (FLIR TAU2) supports three AGC modes: automatic, manual and linear (linear histogram). Automatic mode is most sophisticated for most situations. In this mode, image contrast and brightness are optimized automatically. In the manual mode, image contrast and brightness are controlled manually using   corresponding   sliders   (on   the   right   side).   In   linear   mode,   mage   contrast   and   brightness   are   optimized automatically on scene using a linear transfer function.  Do FFC (Flat Field Correction)   – FFC is used to correct non-uniformity of the IR image. Camera should be pointed on the uniform area with max zoom (140Z) before pressing this button.  DDE (Digital Detail Enhancement) –   DDE is typically used in the automatic AGC mode where only one threshold is used to determine the enhancement on a per scene basis. DDE range from -20 to -1 soften the image, zero is off and 1 to 100 sharpens the image and helps accent edges and other high frequency spatial details.  ACE (Active Contrast Enhancement)   – The feature provides a contrast adjustment dependent on the relative scene temperature. ACE thresholds greater than 0 impart more contrast to hotter scene content and decrease contrast for colder scene content (e.g. sky or ocean). ACE threshold less than 0 do the opposite by decreasing contrast for hotter scene content and leaving more of the gray-scale shades to represent the colder scene content. ACE can range from -8 to 8 where -8 decreases contrast in darker regions while +8 increases contrast in darker regions.  SSO (Smart Scene Optimization) –   SSO value defines the percentage of the histogram that will be allotted a linear mapping. Enabling this feature facilitates the avoidance of irradiance level compression, which is specifically important for bi-modal scenes. With SSO enabled, the radiometric aspects of an image are better preserved (i.e. the difference in gray shades between two objects is more representative of the difference in temperature). SSO helps to set how linear the HEQ and information based AGC algorithms can be to   provide the highest amount of perceived contrast in every scene. SSO is a percent and can range from 0 to 100%. 7 Maintenance  Page:   7-4  Date:   11.Jun.2021  Rev:   2.0  7.5 Nitrogen Purging  This procedure is meant for purging the humid air out of the gimbal, and fill it with inert gas (99,999% Nitrogen). Without this procedure the humid air inside the camera can cause water condensation on internal components as a result of temperature changes. This can cause blurry image and in the worst case electrical and mechanical component damage and corrosion. For purging Epsilon 140Z 6 mm PUN tube can be directly inserted in to the port. Epsilon 140,140LC and 140Z G2 have a special purging two-way adapter that should be attached in order to ensure Epsilon ir completely purged. Two-way purging adapter should be attached when purging setup is already adjusted. Purging procedure should be done very carefully, to avoid any accidental overpressure bursts that could cause damage to the camera.
